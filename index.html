Modulation Matrix Simulator

⚠️ Volume Warning / Cuidado con el Volumen

Modulation routings can create rapid amplitude shifts and resonant filter peaks. Please set your headphones or speakers to a comfortable, moderate level before starting.

Objective: Make a static sound move by routing modulation sources (LFO, Velocity, Mod Wheel) to targets (Pitch, Loudness, Filter Cutoff).

Instructions & Real-time Controls:

  1. Click Start Simulator to initialize the engine.
  2. Press and hold Spacebar or the on-screen button to trigger the sound.
  3. Live Gestures (Option A): You can adjust sliders (such as the Mod Wheel or Velocity) while holding the note to hear real-time parameter changes.
  4. Complete the 5 sequential missions to recreate classic musical effects: Vibrato, Tremolo, Wah, and Dynamic Velocity.

Control Flow: Making a Sound Move

Connect a source (what moves) to a target (what gets changed). Live adjustments are active while holding the note.
